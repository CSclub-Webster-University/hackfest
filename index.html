THIS YEAR'S THEME

Sustainability & The Future

Teams can choose one of the prompts below to solve, or use them as inspiration to define their own problem within the theme.

SECTION A Broad & Vague

Creative freedom — interpret the theme however you see fit.

  1. "The Last Battery" – In a future where energy is the rarest resource, how do people live, trade, and survive?
  2. "Nature Reclaims the Server" – Build something that explores the intersection of high-technology and organic growth.
  3. "The Weight of a Click" – Every digital action has a physical cost. Make that cost visible, impactful, or part of a world-building mechanic.
  4. "Generation 100" – Design a system or story that focuses on how actions today affect people living exactly 100 years from now.
  5. "The Silent Forecast" – Address the "invisible" data of climate change. How can we visualize or interact with the environmental impact we cannot see?
  6. "Digital Architecture" – Build a framework or world where digital structures are not permanent but grow, evolve, and decay like biological organisms.
  7. "Echoes of the Anthropocene" – Focus on the "artifacts" we leave behind. Create a project centered on the repurposing of human-made ruins in a post-sustainability world.

SECTION B Targeted & Specific

Focused solutions with clear technical or business angles.

  1. CSCBIZ The Ghost Energy Solution: Identify and "gamify" the reduction of standby power in residential or campus buildings.
  2. GD The Micro-Climate Simulator: Create an experience that teaches users about the delicate balance of a specific local ecosystem through direct interaction.
  3. BIZCSC The Circular Campus: Develop a platform centered around a "Zero-Waste" inventory system where items must be repaired or traded.
  4. The Transparent Supply Chain: Solve the problem of "Greenwashing." Create a way for consumers to verify the actual environmental impact of a product.
  5. CSC The Low-Power Web: Build a functional tool that helps users browse the web using the absolute minimum amount of server energy possible.
  6. CSCBIZ The Smart Grid Neighborhood: Develop a peer-to-peer energy sharing dashboard that allows neighbors to trade excess solar or wind power locally.
  7. CSC E-Waste Lifecycle Optimizer: Build a tool that scans hardware components to identify their remaining lifespan and connects users to recycling centers.

DIVERSIFIERS

Optional bonus challenges. Points are added on top of your 300-point total. You must declare your chosen diversifiers in your trial submission on Saturday night.

Level 1 — The Basics +1 Point Each
The Eco-Aesthetic

Incorporate a color palette or visual motif inspired by a specific, real-world endangered biome.

Biomimicry Basics

Name your project, characters, variables, or business tiers entirely after local flora and fauna.

The Ripple Effect

Every major action the user takes must visibly or audibly trigger a minor change in a completely different part of the system or UI.

CSCGD Dark Mode Default

To conserve screen energy, your application or game must default to a dark mode.

BIZ The Charitable Cut

Your business plan must explicitly map how a percentage of profits directly funds a verified environmental initiative.

"Reduce, Reuse, Recycle"

Use at least one major open-source asset or dataset, and explicitly credit it in your pitch.

Level 2 — Moderate Constraints +2 Points Each
Nature's Metronome

The pacing or timing of your solution/game must be tied to a natural real-world cycle (e.g., day/night, tides).

The Power of One

Contrast the impact of a single individual's choices against a massive system or corporation.

Sunset Clause

The project must have a built-in "end of life" state (e.g., features that auto-delete after their useful lifespan).

GDCSC Asset Recycling

Repurpose the exact same visual asset in at least three completely different, creative ways.

BIZ The "Paperless" Pitch

Your Sunday business pitch must be presented without any text on the slides—only images and graphs.

CSC Low-Bandwidth Mode

Your application must include a toggle that drastically reduces data usage.

Level 3 — The Challengers +3 Points Each
"One Man's Trash..."

Make "upcycling" the central mechanic of your code, narrative, and market strategy.

Symbiosis

The core loop requires two users/players to cooperate simultaneously; it cannot be played alone.

The Butterfly Effect

A choice made in the first 60 seconds must drastically alter the final output or ending.

BIZ The Circular Economy

Your model cannot rely on buying "new" things. Users must trade, repair, or upcycle.

CSC Carbon Footprint Tracker

Implement a functional backend feature that calculates the simulated "carbon footprint" of user actions.

GD Show, Don't Tell

Convey the theme of sustainability entirely through mechanics. Zero text or dialogue allowed.

Level 4 — Advanced Engineering +4 Points Each
Degrowth

As the user progresses, the interface or gameplay must become simpler rather than scaling up in complexity.

The 100-Year Plan

The logic, mechanics, or business model must explicitly account for the passage of a century.

CSC Zero-Waste Codebase

No dead code, no unused assets, no console errors, and total project size under 50MB.

BIZCSC Local-First Architecture

Prioritize local offline functionality or peer-to-peer connection to reduce server reliance.

GD The Ephemeral UI

No permanent HUD. Information must be conveyed organically through the environment.

⚔️ Level 5 — The Iron Tier +5 Points Each
Organic Intelligence (The "No AI" Run)

Absolutely zero use of Generative AI for code, art, text, or business ideation. 100% human-crafted.

The Analog Fallback

Your digital solution must be accompanied by a fully functional, physical, non-digital version (e.g., a board game).

Total Transparency

Every background calculation or business fee must always be fully visible to the user on the main screen.

CSC The E-Waste Savior

Project must run flawlessly under extreme low-memory/low-CPU constraints (e.g., 512MB RAM restriction).

The One-Input Constraint

Entire project must be navigable/playable using only a single input method (e.g., one button).

BIZ The True Cost

Your model must mathematically prove how the business achieves "Net Zero" carbon output, accounting for wattage and manufacturing.

JUDGING

What to expect on Sunday at 3:00 PM — here's exactly how the judging expo will work so you can prepare your pitches.

The 4-Squad System

You will stay at your team's table, and the judges will come to you. You will be visited by four different squads of true experts:

💻 Tech Squad A
💻 Tech Squad B
🎨 Creative Design Squad
📈 Business Strategy Squad

Your final Tech score is the average of both Tech Squad visits.

The 10-Minute Drill

Every time a squad visits your table, you have exactly 10 minutes:

3 MIN Give your core pitch & demo
5 MIN Q&A — tailor your answers to the squad at your table
2 MIN The squad scores your project and moves on

You will pitch 4 separate times. Drink some water, hype each other up, and treat the 4th pitch with as much energy as the 1st.
